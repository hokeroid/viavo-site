**Short answer.** Since iOS 26.4, ChatGPT and other AI chatbots run in CarPlay as voice chats: good to talk to, but when ZDNET tested ChatGPT and Perplexity, neither played music, made a call or sent a message, and ChatGPT couldn't plan a route because it can't see your location. Viavo understands plain speech in 29 languages and does those things: it opens the route in your navigator, plays your music, calls (on iOS 27 also inside WhatsApp) and reads and answers your messages.

## Which chatbots are in CarPlay

Apple opened CarPlay to voice chat apps in iOS 26.4. ChatGPT arrived at the end of March 2026, then Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI, Claude and Microsoft Copilot. Google brought the Gemini app to CarPlay on 11 November 2026; its announcement lists conversation, your calendar, email and places on the way, not calls, messages or music. In October Google began rolling out Gemini in Google Maps on CarPlay, first in the US.

They are good company on a long drive: ask about anything, think out loud, learn something. OpenAI's own words about ChatGPT in CarPlay: it "can't access maps, vehicle data, live location, or control other apps."

## What they couldn't do in a real test

ZDNET tried ChatGPT and Perplexity as CarPlay assistants (May 2026). ChatGPT couldn't give directions or find places nearby, because it doesn't know where you are. Perplexity could open a route in Apple Maps and work with the calendar and reminders. Neither played music, and neither made calls or sent messages.

So the obvious advice is "use the chatbot to talk and Siri to drive". That works if Siri understands you. As of November 2026 Siri doesn't speak Polish, Czech, Slovak, Romanian, Greek, Hungarian, Croatian, Serbian or Ukrainian, and the new Siri AI isn't available on iPhone in the EU.

What Viavo doesn't do: the steering-wheel button stays with Siri, so Viavo starts with one tap on the car screen, or when you say "Viavo, listen" to Siri (iOS 26 or later). Before iOS 27, a WhatsApp or SMS draft waits for your tap on the phone. YouTube videos start on the phone, not on the car screen.

## How does WhatsApp work with Viavo?

Works on iOS 27 or later, through the one Viavo automation. On earlier iOS versions, a message opens ready on your phone and you tap Send.

Viavo reads aloud new WhatsApp and SMS messages that arrive after you connect: the sender and the notification’s preview text, not past chats or attachments. SMS with one-time codes and messages from short numbers are skipped.

A reply goes out only after you confirm the person and the text. On WhatsApp, Viavo replies to people who have written to you since you connected; an SMS can go to anyone in your contacts, and the phone fills in the number itself. “Call Anna on WhatsApp” starts a WhatsApp call to anyone saved in your contacts, from the car screen too.

While parked, open Viavo’s Settings and follow the steps: install the Viavo shortcut, turn on its automation and run the check. The same automation covers WhatsApp, SMS, calls in WhatsApp and Viber, and opens Viavo when CarPlay connects.

## Questions

**Can ChatGPT in CarPlay start navigation?** No. OpenAI says it can't access maps or your location or control other apps. Viavo opens the route in the navigator you use.

**Can ChatGPT in CarPlay send a WhatsApp message or play Spotify?** No. Viavo plays music in the app you use, and on iOS 27 it reads new WhatsApp messages aloud, replies by voice and starts WhatsApp calls, with the setup and recipient limits described above. On earlier iOS the message opens ready on your phone.

**Does Viavo replace Siri?** Not entirely. The steering-wheel button stays with Siri. Viavo is one tap on the car screen, or "Viavo, listen" said to Siri (iOS 26 or later), and after that you talk normally.

**Is Viavo free?** You can try it for free: a new account starts with free credits or a free week of Viavo Plus (one free week per person). After that, Viavo Plus is a monthly or yearly subscription.

**Which languages?** 29, each with a native voice, including nine that Siri doesn't speak: Polish, Czech, Slovak, Romanian, Greek, Hungarian, Croatian, Serbian and Ukrainian.
