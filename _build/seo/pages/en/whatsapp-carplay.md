A new WhatsApp message comes in as you pull away. Open Viavo on CarPlay and ask, “Who messaged me?” To answer, try “Tell Anna I’ll be ten minutes late.” Listen for her name and your message before you confirm. Voice replies need iOS 27 or later.

[Download on the App Store]

## Try one message while parked

While parked, open Viavo’s Settings and follow the steps: install the Viavo shortcut, turn on its automation and run the check. The same automation covers WhatsApp, SMS, calls in WhatsApp and Viber, and opens Viavo when CarPlay connects.

Connect CarPlay, then ask a friend to send you a private WhatsApp text. Try a short question such as “Where should we meet?” Ask Viavo to read it and dictate a meeting place. Have your friend check the reply.

## Why can I see the chat on my phone but hear nothing in the car?

Viavo uses the preview of a new notification. An older conversation, a photo or a voice note won’t give you a text preview to test. Leave the car connected and ask for a fresh text.
