Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026. Applies to the Viavo app for iPhone and CarPlay (bundle app.viavo.drive).

Viavo is a voice assistant. To do what you ask, it has to hear you, understand you and reach the apps and data on your phone. This page says exactly what leaves your phone, where it goes, how long we keep it and how to delete it. We do not sell your data and we show no advertising.

1. Who is responsible

Viavo is developed and operated by its developer (“we”). Contact us through the Support page.

2. Your account

You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive the identifier that provider gives us, your e-mail address (with Sign in with Apple it may be a private relay address) and, if provided, your name. The account keeps your settings and data together across your devices and lets us apply fair usage limits. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth.

3. Your voice

Recording starts only when you tap the microphone button, on the phone or on the CarPlay screen, and stops after you finish speaking or after 30 seconds. There is no wake word and no listening in the background.

  • Server recognition (default). The recording is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and transcribed with OpenAI’s speech-to-text service. We do not keep the audio after the transcript is produced.
  • On-device recognition. In Settings you can switch to recognition on the phone. The app then downloads a speech model (from Hugging Face, about 500 MB) and your audio never leaves your phone; only the text does.

4. Understanding and answering

The text of your request is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic’s Claude model, which works out what you want and writes the answer. For questions about current facts or places, the model may run a web search; your request text is what is searched for. The answer is turned into speech by ElevenLabs (or OpenAI when ElevenLabs is unavailable); only the answer text is sent to them.

To answer well, each request carries some context from your phone:

  • your location (coordinates from the phone’s location service), time zone and app language, and the home and work addresses you saved in Settings, so the assistant can find places near you and route you home. Coordinates are used only to answer the request and are not stored; a bug report keeps only the city and country;
  • which navigator and music app you chose, which apps are installed and which permissions are off, so the assistant can say what it can and cannot do;
  • what is playing now, when you ask about music;
  • your upcoming calendar events and reminders, when the request concerns them.

Messengers. If you connect Telegram or Slack in Settings and ask about your messages (“what did Anna write?”, “tell the team I’m late”), our server reads or sends, on your behalf and only at that moment, what you asked for, and passes the text of the fetched messages to the language model so it can answer. Voice messages are transcribed by OpenAI. The access to your account (the Telegram session, the Slack token) is encrypted with a key kept only on the server and stored on your phone, never on the server; the fetched messages are not stored. The assistant’s spoken retelling is kept as part of the day’s conversation like any reply. You agree to this on the Messengers screen before connecting, and “Disconnect” ends it. The mechanics are described in detail in How Viavo handles your messages.

Weather questions send your coordinates to Open-Meteo. Podcast searches send the search text to Apple’s iTunes Search API. Places are looked up with Apple Maps on your phone under Apple’s own privacy terms.

6. Permissions on your phone

Viavo asks for each permission when you first need it and works without any of them except the microphone. Microphone: to hear you. Location: for routes, places and weather. Contacts: to call by name. Apple Music: to play from your library. Calendar and Reminders: to create and read events and reminders. HomeKit: to control accessories. You can change any permission in Viavo Settings → Permissions or in the iPhone Settings app.

7. Apps Viavo opens

When you ask for a route or music, Viavo opens the app you chose (Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, Yandex Maps or Navigator, Apple Music, Spotify, Apple Podcasts) and passes it the destination or the search text. From that point the other app’s privacy policy applies.

9. Deleting your data

Signing out removes the session from the phone. To delete your account and everything in section 5, use “Delete account” in Viavo Settings, or ask us via the Support page and we will do it within 30 days. Contact and music data is replaced on each sync and removed with the account.

10. Your rights

If you are in the EU, the UK or another place with data protection laws, you can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, and object to processing. Our legal bases are performing the service you asked for and, for phone permissions, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Write to us via the Support page.

11. Children

Viavo is for drivers and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes

When this policy changes we update the date at the top. Significant changes are announced in the app.
