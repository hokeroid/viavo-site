The assistant can misunderstand you or give a wrong answer: check anything that matters (addresses, times, what a message says) before you rely on it, and never let the app distract you from driving. Every request to the assistant is paid for in credits. A request that the assistant answers or carries out costs 1 credit; a request that needed a web search for current facts costs 3 credits. Short feedback about Viavo itself (“report a bug”, “I wish Viavo could…”) is charged the same way, up to three times a day.

A new account receives 10 credits as a gift. They never expire and are spent before any other credits.
